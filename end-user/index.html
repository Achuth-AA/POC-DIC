About the scheme

PM-KISAN provides income support to landholding farmer families across the country to help them meet expenses related to agriculture and allied activities, as well as domestic needs.

The benefit of ₹6,000 per year is transferred in three four-monthly instalments of ₹2,000 each through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts of beneficiaries.

State Governments and Union Territory Administrations identify the eligible farmer families and upload their details for verification.

Instalment cycle

  1. April – July1st instalment of the year
  2. August – November2nd instalment of the year
  3. December – March3rd instalment of the year

Who is eligible

  • Landholding farmer families with cultivable land in their names, as per land records of the State/UT.
  • A family means husband, wife and minor children.
  • Bank account seeded with Aadhaar and e-KYC completed.

Who is not eligible

  • Institutional landholders.
  • Present or former holders of constitutional posts, ministers, MPs, MLAs and similar elected representatives.
  • Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Governments and PSUs (except Multi Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D).
  • Retired pensioners with a monthly pension of ₹10,000 or more (except Multi Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D).
  • Persons who paid income tax in the last assessment year.
  • Registered professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants and architects.

How to apply

  1. 1

    Keep documents ready

    Aadhaar card, land ownership records, bank account details and a mobile number linked to Aadhaar.

  2. 2

    Register

    Use "New farmer registration" on the portal, visit a Common Service Centre (CSC), or contact the state nodal officer or local patwari.

  3. 3

    Verification

    The State/UT verifies land records and Aadhaar; the bank account is validated before payment.

  4. 4

    Complete e-KYC

    OTP-based e-KYC on the portal, or biometric e-KYC at a CSC.

Frequently asked questions

How much money does a farmer family receive?

₹6,000 per year, paid as three instalments of ₹2,000 each.

Is e-KYC mandatory?

Yes. e-KYC must be completed to continue receiving instalments. It can be done with an OTP on the portal or biometrically at a CSC.

My instalment has not arrived. What should I do?

Check your beneficiary status, make sure e-KYC is complete and your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar. If the problem continues, register a grievance.

Can I update my mobile number or bank details?

Yes. Contact your state nodal officer or a CSC, or raise a grievance with the correct details.
